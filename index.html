        PixelWorld

        One pixel canvas for the whole internet. Everyone who opens this page paints on the same 2400 × 1350 grid, live. Turn your phone sideways to see all of it.

        How it works

        Pick a colour and draft pixels: tap them in Move mode, or drag across the canvas in Draw mode. Drafts only you can see. When you're happy, press Place and they all go up at once.

        New players start with 16 pixels. One more comes back every 30 seconds, and your bank holds up to 64. Anyone can paint over anyone, so the art keeps changing.

        Tools

        The rainbow swatch opens all 254 colours and a colour wheel. Pick copies the colour of any pixel. Mine highlights your pixels that are still showing. Guide lays an image over the canvas to trace.

        Tap any pixel to see who painted it last; tap the line under the tools for its full history, a link to it, or to report it. Tap online now to jump to where someone just painted. When you're zoomed in, the minimap in the corner shows where you are; tap it to jump.

        It never resets

        Every pixel ever placed is kept. The timelapse replays the whole history, from a blank canvas to right now.

        No accounts or passwords. A username is optional and shows as "by your name" on pixels you paint; without one you're anonymous. Your device gets an anonymous ID so the pixel bank is fair.
