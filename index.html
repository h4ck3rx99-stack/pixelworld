PixelWorld

One pixel canvas for the whole internet. Everyone who opens this page paints on the same 1200 × 1800 grid, live.

How it works

Pick one of 16 colours and draft pixels: tap them in Move mode, or drag across the canvas in Draw mode. Drafts only you can see. When you're happy, press Place and they all go up at once.

You have up to 64 pixels in your bank, and one more comes back every 30 seconds. Anyone can paint over anyone, so the art keeps changing.

Tools

Pick copies the colour of any pixel. Mine highlights your pixels that are still showing. Tap any pixel to see how often it's been painted and when. Pinch or double-tap to zoom; two fingers always pan.

It never resets

Every pixel ever placed is kept. The timelapse replays the whole history, from a blank canvas to right now.

No accounts, no names, no text. Your device gets an anonymous ID so the pixel bank is fair.
