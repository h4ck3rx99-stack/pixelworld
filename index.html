RangoliLive

One rangoli for the whole internet, drawn live by everyone who opens this page.

How the symmetry works

The floor is cut into 8 identical wedges, 4 turns and their mirror images. When you tap, your piece lands in one cell and is drawn in all 8 wedges at once. So even random taps by strangers turn into a pattern, and nobody can draw anything rude.

The rules

One piece every 4 seconds. A new piece replaces whatever was in that cell. The centre lotus takes the colour of whoever tapped it last.

Every day is new

The board clears at midnight IST. Each day's rangoli is kept in the gallery with a timelapse of how it was drawn.

No accounts, no names, no text. Your device gets an anonymous ID so the 4 second rule is fair.
